The draft is a verbatim capture from the seller's own eBayListing folder (see
AmazonListingFillFixtures). The product type schema is the reconstruction in
AmazonProductTypeFixtures - this deployment cannot obtain an Amazon access token,
so no schema here came off Amazon's wire, and the SP-API sandbox answers every
product type query with luggage regardless of the keywords. What is proven below
is the JOIN: which of the AI's already-extracted fields answer which Amazon
attributes, what payload comes out, and what happens where nothing answers.

EITHER/OR REQUIREMENTS (0 of 1 satisfied)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [BLOCKED] Amazon needs one of these and the draft has none: External Product ID, Merchant Suggested ASIN.

  [missing] externally_assigned_product_identifier     —
           The draft has no UPC, EAN or ISBN. Amazon will not create a new
           listing without a product identifier unless your account holds a
           GTIN exemption for the brand. Find the real barcode or apply for
           the exemption — a made-up one is an account suspension, not a
           rejected listing.
  [missing] merchant_suggested_asin                    —
           The ASIN of an existing Amazon catalogue entry. Nothing in an eBay
           draft is one, and a guessed ASIN attaches this offer to somebody
           else's product.
